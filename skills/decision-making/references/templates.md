# Decision-making templates

Fill these in with the user, in writing.

## 1. Decision brief (phases 1 to 4)

```
Decision:
Date:            Decide by:          Who decides:

Triage
  Reversibility (easy / costly / hard):
  Cost of being wrong:
  How long it lasts:

Frame
  Goal (one sentence):
  How I will know I achieved it:
  Real problem (cause, not symptom):
  Constraints / non-negotiables:
  Options (include: do nothing, smaller, later, hybrid):

Gather
  Limits set in advance (sources / time):
  Sources: who / direct experience? / what they gain / what they said
  Heard from people who chose differently? yes / no
  Facts (checked):
  Opinions and claims (unverified):
  Still unknown:

Stress-test
  Assumptions that must hold (verified? how to check cheaply):
  Pre-mortem: if this fails a year from now, most likely because:
  Later consequences of each option:
  Worst case, and can I live with it:
  Way out, and its cost:

Choose
  Dealbreakers:
  Options removed and why:
  Comparison (criteria / weight / option scores):
  Emotion present? Waited until:
  Intuition used? (real experience in this kind of decision: yes / no)
  Decision:
  Reasons:
  Fallback:
```

## 2. Commitment note (phase 5, before starting)

```
What I will do differently:
What stays the same:
Success measure and expected result:
Minimum run length or number of tries before judging:
Checkpoint date (in the calendar):
Exit trigger (if ___ by ___, I stop or change course):
Start date:
Running alongside the old way? how:
Until the checkpoint I will not change: ___

Result log: date of action / result
Doubt log: date / what I felt / what the numbers said
```

## 3. Checkpoint review (phase 6)

One decision per sheet. Describe first, then fix.

```
DESCRIBE
Decision and date:
Goal and problem as I framed them:
What I expected, by when:
What actually happened (from the record):
Enough time or data to judge?  yes / no
Did it work?  ___ / 10

Outside causes ruled out?
  One-off event:
  Conditions changed:
  Inputs worse than planned:
  Execution drifted from plan:

If none explain it, earliest gap in the process
(goal / framing / sources / unchecked claim / emotion / skipped test / commitment):

DECIDE WHAT TO DO
Verdict: not yet | not a mistake (outside causes) | mistake in the process (which)
Response sized to the miss: more of the same / small adjustment / larger change / different approach
Change (one thing) and version number:
One lesson:
Line to add to the framing of my next decision:
```

Keep finished sheets together and reread their lessons before the next big decision.
